# SkullClick privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-21_

SkullClick does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

- **No network requests.** The extension doesn't contact any server: no analytics, telemetry,
  crash reports, ads or remote configuration.
- **What is stored, and where.** Your settings (whether Grudge mode is on) are kept in the
  browser's extension storage and may be synced by your browser's own sync feature if you use it.
  The Grudge mode hit list (website host names, page paths and CSS selectors of the elements you
  removed) is stored only locally in your browser. It never leaves your device unless you export it
  to a file yourself.
- **Permissions.**
  - *activeTab* and *scripting*: to show the element picker on the tab where you click the
    SkullClick button or press its shortcut.
  - *storage*: to keep your settings and hit list.
  - *Access to all websites* (optional, requested only when you turn on Grudge mode): to hide
    remembered elements automatically as pages load. SkullClick doesn't read or record page content.
- **Removing your data.** Use *Delete all* in the options page, or uninstall the extension,
  which deletes everything it stored.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Perruer/skullclick/issues
